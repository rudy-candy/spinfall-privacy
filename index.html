Privacy Policy — Spinfall (メダルフィーバー)

Last updated: 2026-11-10

This policy explains how the mobile game Spinfall ("the App") handles information. The App is developed and published by an individual developer (contact below).

1. Information we collect

None. The App has no account system, no login, no advertising, no analytics, and no server of our own. We do not collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, or any device identifier.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only (your medal count, the jackpot meter, and whether you have finished your first Fever Game). It contains no personal information. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data from Android settings.

3. No real money

Medals in the App are for play only. The App does not use real money, has no in-app purchases, and medals cannot be exchanged for money, prizes, or anything of value.

4. Third parties

The App does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not share or sell any information with third parties. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

Updated versions will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact
